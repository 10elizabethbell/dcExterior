# PRODUCT.md — DC Exterior

- **Platform:** web
- **Stack:** static single-file HTML/CSS/JS (GitHub Pages), Cal Sans from Google Fonts, no frameworks, no tracking
- **Surface mode:** Persuade (one-page site for a local trade)

## Users
- Homeowners who notice a leak, a loose gutter, crumbling mortar or a damaged chimney after wet weather and want someone to look at it. They arrive on a phone, mostly from Facebook (in-app browser).
- The owner of DC Exterior, who gets the site for free and has to recognise their own business in it within one screen.

## Purpose
Turn "I saw your post" into a free-quote request the owner actually receives. Today their only funnel is a Facebook group ad offering "Free quotes" with no phone, email or form attached.

## Positioning
An exterior contractor for the parts of a house that weather attacks first: roof, chimney, gutters, pointing/brickwork, plus exterior painting (on their logo). Their own line: "Protecting Your Property in Every Season."

## Capabilities and constraints
- Contact is Facebook Messenger only (no phone or email published). The quote form composes a message and opens Messenger (`m.me/61558693145112`), with a copy-message fallback.
- No service area, address, hours, owner name, prices, reviews or work photos are published. None are invented on the page.
- The page must work inside Facebook's in-app browser on cellular.

## Brand commitments
- Logo: white "DC" / "EXTERIOR" with a bright blue (#038df8) house, roller and gutter on black (Facebook profile image). Shown on navy, never on a black ground.
- Ad colours: navy band (#04203a), yellow headline (#f8d404), red roof icon.
- Voice: "we / us" (the ad says "Contact us"). Problem-first, seasonal: "Wet Weather Shows Up All Kinds of Issues".

## Evidence on hand
- Facebook profile logo (720x720).
- One ad graphic from the 2026-10-20 group post: an illustrated house (not a photo of their work) labelled with Roof, Chimney, Gutters, Pointing and the signs of each.
- Logo tagline: "Painting · Gutters · Roof Repairs".

## Principles
- Phone first; the first screen shows who, what and the free-quote action.
- Only facts from the brief, the ad and the logo. Inferences are listed in HANDOFF.md.
- Calm band style (paper/navy bands, one yellow accent as a fill, Cal Sans headings).
